B STAR WEBSITE — BLUE THEME

OPEN LOCALLY
1. Extract All from the ZIP.
2. Open the extracted B-Star-Website folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
No Python, terminal, installation, or local server is required.
Keep all files and folders together.

PAGES
Home: South East Asian Cuisine, NY Times article link, three photographed dishes.
Menu: dinner, lunch/brunch, drinks, and $29 prix fixe, transcribed from the four supplied menus. Original menu images can be enlarged.
Private Dining: expanded gallery and an on-page event inquiry form with validation and review.
Our Story and Visit: straightforward restaurant information.

EVENT INQUIRY DELIVERY
The form collects and reviews details on the page. It does not automatically send or store submissions. Guests can open a populated email draft addressed to eventsatbstar@gmail.com or download the inquiry. They must send it themselves. No fake sent confirmation is shown.
An actual form delivery service or backend must be connected before automatic submission is offered to customers.

NOTES
Internet is needed for online booking, online ordering, external article links, Google Maps, and Google Fonts. Local pages, photos, menu browsing, and form review work directly from the extracted folder.
The NY Times article is linked as reading about Bay Area Burmese cuisine. No quote or restaurant endorsement is asserted.
Photos are sourced from restaurant listings and the official B Star website; image-sources.json records newly added asset URLs.
Menus follow supplied images. Confirm prices, hours and event capacity with the restaurant before public launch.
