OUR RESTAURANT

Burmese favorites.
Regional variety.

B Star’s menu brings together Burmese dishes and flavors from across Asia. Tea leaf salad, chicken coconut curry noodles, and garlic-chili cauliflower are a few places to start.

Our kitchen draws inspiration from Burma, Thailand, China, India, Korea, Japan, the Philippines, and California. The menu includes vegetarian options, lunch dishes, and weekend brunch.

Visit us for dinner, order takeout, or plan a group event in our enclosed patio.
